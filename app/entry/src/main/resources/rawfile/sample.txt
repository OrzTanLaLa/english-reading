Grit is not simply persistence. It is the tendency to sustain interest in, and effort toward, very long-term goals. A student who studies for a test is being diligent; a student who studies for a decade is something else entirely. Researchers have found that grit predicts success more reliably than talent does, which is why so many schools have begun to teach it — though whether a trait like this can be taught at all remains an open question.
